# CS-250 Portfolio: SNHU Travel Agile Project

## Reflection Questions

### 1. How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating "user stories" help with this?
I interpret user needs by analyzing what features end users require and breaking them down into manageable, actionable pieces. Creating user stories helps bridge the gap between user expectations and technical tasks by utilizing the template: *"As a [user], I want [feature] so that [benefit]."* This approach keeps development focused on deliverable value, clear acceptance criteria, and user satisfaction rather than just technical requirements.

### 2. How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?
I approach program development through an iterative, collaborative framework. Working in short cycles (sprints) allows for continuous testing, feedback, and flexibility. In my future development work, I hope to incorporate core Scrum events like Daily Standups for team alignment, Sprint Reviews for early stakeholder feedback, and Retrospectives to continuously refine team efficiency and communication.

### 3. What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?
Being a good team member means maintaining active, honest, and transparent communication. It involves taking ownership of tasks, proactively signaling blockers during standups, being receptive to constructive feedback, and supporting teammates to achieve shared sprint goals.
